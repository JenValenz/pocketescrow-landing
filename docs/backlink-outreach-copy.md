# Pocket Escrow App — Backlink Outreach Copy

Reusable copy for directory submissions, guest-post pitches, and trade
association outreach. See the prioritized target list in the SEO plan
(Search Console → author page → glossary → wire fraud page → backlinks).

## Taglines (for name/tagline fields — G2, Capterra, Product Hunt)

- **Short (≤60 char):** California escrow education, in your pocket.
- **Alt:** Escrow, decoded — for buyers, sellers, and pros.

## Descriptions, by length

**Micro (~25 words — directory one-liners)**
> Pocket Escrow App is a California real estate escrow education platform: a 177-term glossary, interactive timelines, calculators, and fillable forms, built by a 30-year escrow professional.

**Short (~60 words — G2/Capterra "about," social bios)**
> Pocket Escrow App demystifies California real estate escrow for buyers, sellers, borrowers, realtors, loan officers, and title agents. Built by Jennifer Valenzuela, a 30-year California escrow professional and DFPI-Qualified Escrow Manager, it includes a 177-term glossary, interactive purchase/refinance timelines, a Seller Net Sheet and Buyer Closing Costs calculator, 19 fillable forms, and an AI assistant trained on California escrow.

**Long (~150 words — Product Hunt, full directory profiles, guest-post bylines)**
> Escrow is one of the least understood steps in buying, selling, or refinancing a home — despite being the point where the money actually changes hands. Pocket Escrow App was built to close that gap.
>
> Founded by Jennifer Valenzuela, a 30-year California escrow professional, DFPI-Qualified Escrow Manager, and founder of three licensed California escrow companies, Pocket Escrow App gives buyers, sellers, and borrowers a plain-English reference for every step of their transaction — and gives realtors, loan officers, escrow officers, and title agents a practical daily toolkit.
>
> Free (Guest tier) access includes a 177-term glossary and interactive timelines. Paid tiers add 19 fillable escrow forms, a Seller Net Sheet and Buyer Closing Costs calculator, an AI chat assistant trained on California escrow, and Property Profile lookups.
>
> Built for California. Education, not legal advice.
