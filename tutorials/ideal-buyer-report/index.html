Ideal Buyer Report

The Ideal Buyer Report tells you who is most likely to buy a listing, and how to talk to them. Here is how it works. Open the Buyer tab in Pocket Es-Crow App.

0:10

Your Company Profile

Step one is your company profile. Enter your company name, phone number, escrow officer, license, and address. Save it once, and every report you generate comes back branded with your details.

0:23

Property Details

Step two is the property. Enter the address and the list price. Those two fields are all that is required.

0:30

School District + HOA

From there, the report fills in the rest. It looks up the school district that serves the property, and whether there is a homeowners association, so you do not have to.

0:40

Key Features

Add key features if you want a sharper result. A pool, a remodeled kitchen, a corner lot.

0:47

Buyer Snapshot

Then generate the report. You get a buyer snapshot first: age range, income range, household, and current situation.

0:56

Value, Motivation, Worries

After that, What They Value. Why They Buy. What Worries Them, with every worry paired to a way you can answer it. Then Talking Points, and Content Ideas you can drop straight into a listing description or a social post.

1:12

Fair Housing Built In

Every report is written to stay inside fair housing rules. No race, no religion, no national origin, ever. Only income, household, motivation, and practical concerns.

1:26

PDF, Email, or In-App

When it is ready, download it as a PDF, email it to your client, or share it inside the app.

1:33

Included on Pro

The Ideal Buyer Report is included on Pro. On any other plan, it is five dollars per report.

1:39

Open the Buyer tab and run one on your next listing.
